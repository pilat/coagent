# Code of conduct

We want participation in coagent to be safe, respectful and productive.
Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experience and
viewpoints, accept technical feedback in good faith, and avoid harassment,
threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing or sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the project or its community. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately and avoid exposing reporters unnecessarily.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-advisory
form at <https://github.com/pilat/coagent/security/advisories/new>. State that
the report concerns community conduct. Do not include unrelated secrets or
publish the report as an issue.

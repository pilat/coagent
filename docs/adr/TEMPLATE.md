## Context

What forces are in play? The technical, product, or operational situation that makes a decision necessary. State the constraints and the tension — what makes this a real tradeoff rather than an obvious call. Keep it to what a reader needs to judge the decision, not a history of the whole system.

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly and in the active voice: "We use X." One decision per ADR. Include the specifics that matter (the version, the boundary, the invariant) so the decision is unambiguous.

## Consequences

What becomes true once this is in place — good and bad. New capabilities, new constraints, things now harder or forbidden, follow-up work created. Be honest about the costs; an ADR that only lists upsides isn't recording a tradeoff.

## Alternatives Considered

The other viable options and **why each was rejected**. This is the part that pays off later: it stops someone from re-litigating a path you already walked. One short paragraph or bullet per alternative.
